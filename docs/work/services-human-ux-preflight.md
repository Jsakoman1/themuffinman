# Services human-understandable UX preflight

Closeout: the verified serial inventory now covers discovery, public mini-site, calendar booking, reviews, documentation, accessibility, and desktop/mobile runtime evidence.

## Starting point

Services already owns public business discovery and favorites. Public profile data,
service schemas, quotes, availability windows, booking preview, booking creation, and
durable customer outcomes exist. The route context repair keeps public business pages
inside Services.

## Residual problem

The current surfaces do not yet read as one coherent customer journey. Discovery is
row-heavy, the public page does not balance identity, gallery, price list, reviews, and
booking, and the date-first flow does not show available days in a real calendar before
a person guesses a date. Business ratings/reviews have no persisted contract yet.

## Authority boundaries

- Backend owns public visibility, availability, timezone, capacity, resources, pricing,
  quote state, preview, booking creation, review eligibility, ratings, permissions, and
  conflicts.
- Web owns local selection, focus, calendar navigation, layout, and presentation.
- Business owner administration, global Calendar, and Vision are baseline-only.
- No payments, map search, staff selection, guest booking, recurrence, threaded review
  comments, owner replies, reporting, or moderation are included.
